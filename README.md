## About Me

I'm a PhD researcher at the **Institut de Robòtica i Informàtica Industrial (IRI)**, a joint centre of CSIC and UPC in Barcelona, where I work in the Perception and Manipulation / LabORA group.

My research focuses on how humans and intelligent robotic systems can communicate, collaborate, and learn from one another. In particular, I work on explainable robotics: developing ways for robots to explain their decisions, actions, and beliefs, and understanding how explanations can support effective and understandable human-robot interaction.

I'm also actively involved in growing and connecting the robotics community across Africa.
